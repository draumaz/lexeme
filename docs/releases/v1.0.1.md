## Lexeme 1.0.1

### Tables that fit
Declension and conjugation tables now fit your screen. Wide tables narrow their columns first and,
if needed, scale down slightly, so most of them fit without scrolling. Tables too wide even then
still scroll sideways, but now there's a clear sign that they do: the edges fade where the table
continues, and a scroll bar underneath shows where you are, with arrows you can tap to move along.

### A tidier toolbar
- The entry toolbar is now just **Home** and **Share** on either side of the search button. Random
  stays on the home screen as **Surprise me**, and links still open Wiktionary in your browser.
- Home, Share and the up button are small spinning scallops in the same frosted glass as the
  search button. Like it, they squish when pressed and spin when tapped.
- The up button now **jumps up one language section** instead of all the way to the top, and only
  appears once you've scrolled a screen or more down the page.

### Feel
- Navigation buttons vibrate when tapped: the toolbar, back buttons, Surprise me, the About links
  in Settings, and "Did you mean" suggestions.
