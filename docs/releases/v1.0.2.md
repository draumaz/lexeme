## Lexeme 1.0.2

### Lookups that load everywhere
On some networks every lookup failed with "Couldn't load this page" and **HTTP 403**: Wikimedia
was turning the app away as an anonymous bot. Lexeme now identifies itself the way Wikimedia asks,
with a link back to the project, and those lookups load again.

### Tablets, foldables and dual screens
On a tablet, an unfolded foldable or a Surface Duo, Lexeme now uses two panes side by side.
- **Entries** put the word and a **contents list** on one side and the entry itself on the other.
  The contents list shows every language on the page, and the one you're reading opens into its
  sections: Etymology, Pronunciation, Noun and so on. It follows along as you scroll, highlighting
  where you are, and tapping any language or section jumps straight there.
- **Home** puts the logo and Surprise me on one side, and your recent words and the search button
  on the other.
- Settings and the search field open in the reading pane, beside what you were looking at.
- On foldables and dual-screen phones the panes split exactly at the fold or hinge, so nothing sits
  on the crease or disappears behind the hinge. Turn the device on its side and they stack top and
  bottom instead.

Phones keep the single-column layout they had.

### Smoother
- Scrolling does less work per frame: the screen is no longer painted several times over before
  anything is drawn, and entries, etymology trees and tables do less redundant drawing.
- The spinning badges and buttons now stop while you scroll, so the page gets the frame time.
  They don't just freeze: they slam to a stop with a little overshoot, then wind back up to speed
  once the page comes to rest.

### Fixes
- Scrolling back up slowly now brings Home and Share back. Before, only a quick flick did.
- Opening a word you'd already looked up now records its real title in Recent, rather than the
  spelling you typed (for example "water", not "Water").
- TalkBack now announces the Material You setting as a switch.
