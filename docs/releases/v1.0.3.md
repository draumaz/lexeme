## Lexeme 1.0.3

### Category pages
`Category:` links now open in Lexeme instead of the browser.
- Each category shows Wiktionary's description, the trail of categories above it, its
  subcategories, and its parent categories. Its pages load in batches as you scroll.
- The categories at the end of an entry are grouped by language.
- Long lists in the Latin, Cyrillic or Greek alphabet (100 pages or more) get an **A–Z sidebar**.
  Drag along it to jump by letter: a bubble shows the letter, with a tick for each one you pass.
  Letters that are already loaded scroll as you drag. Letters further on load the list from that
  letter, labelled "Pages from M", with a button back to the start.

### More than words
Search now finds **Category**, **Reconstruction**, **Thesaurus** and **Appendix** pages as well as
entries. Typing "English nouns" or "Proto-Germanic/wat" finds them, and Enter opens the closest
real match rather than one of the search engine's looser guesses. These pages appear under their
plain name with a small label above it, such as "Reconstruction · Proto-Germanic" over "\*watōr",
in suggestions, page headers and Recent.

### Find on page
A new **Find on page** button sits just above the search button. It opens a bar above the
keyboard that counts and highlights every match on the page. Stepping through them scrolls each
match into view, and opens any section, fold or "show all" list hiding it.

### Select and copy
Press and hold any reading text to select and copy it, one section at a time.

### Pictures
- When an entry has several photos, they show in a **carousel** you can swipe through, with each
  caption beneath its photo. Single pictures and line art get compact cards.
- Picture galleries that sit next to each other merge into one, so pictures no longer stack up
  above the definitions. Picture dictionaries become a single gallery of sharp, captioned images.
- Tap a picture to open it full size. Tap anywhere or go back to close it.

### Getting around
- The bottom bar's Home button is now **Back**. One page in, it still shows the Home icon. Deeper
  in, it shows a back arrow and steps back one page. Press and hold it to go straight home.
- The search button tucks away while you read down a page, and comes back when you scroll up or
  stop scrolling for a moment.
- The search bar that was pinned to the top of entries is gone. Pages now scroll up under the
  status bar, and the language picker stays pinned just below it.
- On tablets and foldables, each language in the contents list now starts collapsed. Tap the
  arrow to open it, or tap the language to jump there.

### Looks
- On Android 17 QPR2 and later, the language picker and the A–Z sidebar are frosted glass. On the
  home screen, a blur deepens smoothly toward the bottom behind your recent words. Older versions
  of Android get solid colours instead.
- The home screen logo and wordmark stay in place however many recent words you have. Extra rows
  run down under the blur.
- Press and hold the logo to wind it up until it pops.
- The Settings back button moves like the Settings button on the home screen.
- The Lexeme wordmark is a little lighter.

### Removed
- **Surprise me** is gone.

### Under the hood
- Lookups are cancelled as soon as you leave a page, instead of finishing in the background.
- Updated to Gradle 9.9.2, Android Gradle Plugin 9.5.2, Compose BOM 2026.11.01 and the stable release
  of Material 3 1.5.0.
